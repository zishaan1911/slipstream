Each servo is mounted upside down through a cut-out in the top plate, so the horn and pushrod run in the gap between the carbon plates, just above the arm. The hinge pin and its plates carry the thrust and crash loads; the servo only has to swing the arm. With the props spinning about a vertical axis and the arm swinging about another vertical axis, folding puts no gyroscopic load on the servo.
